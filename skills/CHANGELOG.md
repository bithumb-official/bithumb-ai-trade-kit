## [0.8.5] - 2026-06-08

### Added

- Initial public release of the Bithumb Skills collection.
- `bithumb-market` — access public market data: prices, candles, orderbook, trades, notices, and fees.
- `bithumb-account` — view balances, trading availability, wallet and withdrawal status, and API key information.
- `bithumb-trade` — place, cancel, and query spot orders, including limit, market, batch, and TWAP orders.
- `bithumb-deposit` — manage deposit addresses, deposit history, and KRW deposit requests.
- `bithumb-withdraw` — manage withdrawals, cancellation, history, fees, and allowed addresses.
- `bithumb-system` — diagnose connectivity, credentials, audit logs, and supported capabilities.
